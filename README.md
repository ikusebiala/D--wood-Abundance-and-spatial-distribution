Deadwood Abundance and Spatial Distribution in Onigambari Forest Reserve
PROJECT BRIEF
Deadwood is an important component of forest ecosystems because standing and fallen dead trees provide habitat and contribute to nutrient cycling. This project will investigate the abundance and spatial distribution of deadwood in Onigambari Forest Reserve, Oyo State, Nigeria.
The study will examine whether deadwood abundance changes with increasing distance from forest edges.
SPATIAL QUESTION
How does deadwood abundance and spatial distribution vary with increasing distance from forest edges in Onigambari Forest Reserve, Nigeria?
STUDY AREA
Onigambari Forest Reserve, Oyo State, Nigeria.
